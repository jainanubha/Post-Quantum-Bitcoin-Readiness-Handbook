## Proposed sequence

Let $H_0$ be a hypothetical activation height for BIP361 after a suitable PQ authorization mechanism is available. Phase A is placed at

$$H_A=H_0+160{,}000.$$

The draft describes Phase B as occurring two years after Phase A at a pre-announced height. Its abstract characterizes the overall period as five years from activation. At a mean 600-second block interval, 160,000 blocks take approximately 1,111 days (3.04 years); this is an expectation rather than a calendar guarantee. The canonical text does not give a precise additional block count for Phase B. A two-year approximation must not be promoted to a consensus constant.

| Period | Conceptual purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Before Phase A | Allow time to adopt a suitable PQ output and migrate |
| Phase A | Stop creating new quantum-vulnerable destinations as described in the draft |
| Phase B | Require stronger authorization for legacy signature spends, including an envisaged rescue condition |

## Revision differences

The project website consulted on November 16, 2026 describes a distinct Phase C for recovery, while its Phase B description is a broader invalidation of legacy signatures. The earlier July 2025 mailing-list proposal also used a three-phase framing. The canonical revision used here instead describes rescue within Phase B. These versions must not be silently combined.

## Dependencies and non-dependencies

BIP360 is related but not a sufficient prerequisite on its own. P2MR commits to a script root and does not introduce PQ verification. A future usable authorization mechanism needs defined algorithms, encodings, signature messages, resource accounting and activation rules. Wallets need safe generation and recovery of compatible outputs; exchanges and services need interoperable payment support. A rescue rule needs its own precisely defined proof and consensus semantics.

The draft expresses an intention to tighten rules through soft forks. The fact that older nodes may continue following a compliant chain does not imply they can independently enforce the new restrictions. A complete specification must define how nodes agree on which outputs and spends are subject to each phase.

## Open issues

- Which outputs qualify as sufficiently protected, and how is that eligibility recognized by consensus?
- What exact height and activation mechanism would govern Phase B?
- What happens if rescue research is incomplete when the transition is scheduled?
