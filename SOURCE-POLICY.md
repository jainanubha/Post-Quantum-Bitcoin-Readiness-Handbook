# Source Policy

This repository links to external papers, BIPs, discussions, standards, articles and implementations. Third-party resources remain under their original licenses and copyrights.

This project does not mirror full third-party papers, standards or articles unless redistribution is explicitly allowed. Summaries, annotations, diagrams and explanations in this repository are original unless otherwise stated.

When adding a new resource, prefer official sources such as:
- bitcoin/bips or bips.dev
- NIST CSRC
- IETF RFCs
- IACR ePrint
- arXiv
- Delving Bitcoin
- bitcoin-dev mailing list
- official project repositories
